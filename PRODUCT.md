# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring teams, prospective clients, and collaborators evaluating Harshit Tanwar's work and fit as a frontend-focused full-stack developer.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Harshit's projects, experience, and capabilities so visitors can assess his work and contact him. Success means visitors can find relevant evidence and a clear way to get in touch.

## Positioning

The current portfolio presents Harshit as a frontend-focused full-stack developer, with an emphasis on motion- and scroll-driven interfaces. Project case studies are the primary way to demonstrate this work; no exclusive market distinction or measured performance claim has been confirmed.

## Operating Context

The site is a public web portfolio with a homepage for work, about, capabilities, experience, and contact, plus project case-study pages. Project content is maintained in a shared data source and used by the homepage and case-study routes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built with Next.js App Router and JavaScript, using Tailwind CSS, GSAP, Framer Motion, and Lenis.
- The site supports responsive layouts, light and dark themes, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion preferences. Preserve these behaviors when changing the site.
- Contact and social links, project details, experience dates, and measured project outcomes must be verified before being presented as facts.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains case-study copy for NRK Rasoma, Fan and Flip, and Careers Interface, plus experience and skills content. Treat these as portfolio content, not independently verified client results or employment claims.
- Project entries do not currently link to live projects or provide measured outcomes.
- The email and social URLs in the site data are explicit placeholders. Do not present them as real contact details.
- No verified testimonials, client endorsements, or performance metrics are currently recorded.

## Product Principles

- Help visitors evaluate relevant work and experience without making them hunt for evidence.
- Keep claims grounded in verifiable project, career, and contact information.
- Make it straightforward for interested visitors to reach out.
